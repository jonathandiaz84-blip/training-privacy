Training app

Privacy Policy

Effective October 9, 2026

This policy covers the optional WHOOP cloud connection in JD's personal Training app.

Data Training accesses

After you explicitly connect WHOOP, Training requests the WHOOP recovery, physiological cycle, sleep, workout, and body measurement scopes. This can include recovery and strain scores, heart rate and HRV, sleep stages and sleep need, workouts and heart rate zones, steps, SpO₂, skin temperature, respiratory rate, weight, height, and profile maximum heart rate.

How the data is used

Training uses this data only to display and analyze your own training, recovery, sleep, workouts, and health trends. Training does not sell it, use it for advertising, or share it with data brokers.

Storage and security

Imported health data is stored in Training's local database on your devices. WHOOP requires a server-side Client Secret, so a private broker on JD's Mac performs the OAuth token exchange and WHOOP API requests. The Client Secret and rotating WHOOP access and refresh tokens remain on that Mac in files restricted to the local user. The iPhone reaches the broker through JD's private Tailscale network over HTTPS.

Retention and deletion

Data remains until it is deleted from Training or the WHOOP connection is removed. Choosing Forget WHOOP access asks WHOOP to revoke access, removes the broker's WHOOP tokens, and deletes the imported WHOOP cloud rows from Training. WHOOP's own retention is governed by the WHOOP Privacy Policy.

Your choices

The WHOOP connection is optional and runs only when you tap Connect or Sync. Training's direct local sensor features continue to work without it.
